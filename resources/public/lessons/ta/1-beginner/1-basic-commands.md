Hello, everyone!

Here, we will draw pictures on the screen.
We do this by giving commands to a turtle.
The turtle is shown as a tiny triangle.
The triangle points in the direction of where the turtle is looking.

The turtle has a pen.
When it moves, it drags the pen with it like a plow, which creates lines and shapes.

---

We can give commands to the turtle.
The turtle will do exactly what we tell it to do.

Here are some basic commands:

* `(forward 50)` - go forwards 50 steps
* `(right 90)` - turn to the right by 90 degrees
* `(left 90)` - turn to the left by 90 degrees
* `(back 50)` - go backwards 50 steps
